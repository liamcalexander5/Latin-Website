The Roman economic system was different from modern systems. Rather than one type of currency, such as the US Dollar, the Romans utilized a variety of coins constructed of different metals. These coins each had different values that fluctuated in worth across different eras of Roman history. This calculator helps you understand the value of your money in ancient Roman terms.

Overall, the Roman economy rose and fell, playing a significant role in the success of the empire as a whole. Trade surplus and low taxation allowed for the common people to thrive and for the empire to grow and expand. However, overreliance on conquest and slaves, overspending on the military, and inflation from debasement of currency all contributed to the eventual decline of the empire. Despite Rome's struggles, it was still one of the most advanced economies of the ancient world, and its legacy can still be seen in our modern financial systems today.
